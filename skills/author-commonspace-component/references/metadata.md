# Metadata checklist

Every stable component metadata entry must include:

- stable ID, name, package, version, category, source path, and public entrypoints;
- specific summary, keywords, use conditions, and avoid conditions;
- accessibility requirements;
- variants and required states;
- tokens and related components or patterns;
- at least one Storybook Story ID;
- preferred and avoid examples when misuse is likely.

Metadata is the canonical AI and documentation source. Do not hand-edit generated DESIGN.md, llms files, or component Markdown.
