# Responsive completion

Design desktop and narrow-screen composition intentionally; do not merely shrink the desktop surface.

- Confirm 320px reflow.
- Let action rows wrap or stack before labels clip.
- Preserve reading and focus order.
- Convert multi-pane workspaces into route or sheet transitions when panes no longer fit.
- Keep feedback and overlays clear of safe areas and fixed navigation.
- Test long Korean and English strings, 200% zoom, dark theme, and high contrast.
